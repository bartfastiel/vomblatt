# Musician's advice (choir director) – basis for the upcoming specs

## Recognition (OMR) minimum

MUST: treble, bass and octave-treble ("tenor", 8 below) clefs; key signature AND accidentals (sharp/flat/natural)
valid to end of bar; time signature incl. C and alla breve; note values incl. dots; all rests; ties; triplets.
Later: dynamics, articulation, ornaments, slurs, divisi (v1: upper note), clef changes mid-system.

## Controls (SATB on 2 or 4 staves)

Must: choose voice S/A/T/B; play/pause/stop; tempo (slower!); start at bar X.
Nice: loop range; own voice louder instead of solo; click; transpose ±semitone.
Leave out: recording, export, multi-user sync.

## Sound & tempo

Piano default (familiar, clear onsets). Organ optional. No sine. Tempo from score if given, else ~80–90 BPM; slowing
down must be instant.

## Pitfalls

Lyrics between staves; repeats & voltas (v1: play linearly, but tell the user); pickup bar shifts bar numbers;
skewed/copied/handwritten sheets need perspective + contrast correction; multiple verses (ignore lyrics); braced
systems; page breaks.

## Honest feedback

Re-render what was recognised, cursor follows playback, mark uncertain spots, tap a note to correct pitch/duration.
